Chaski

El punto de encuentro de los caminantes de Costa Rica

01 — Overview

What is Chaski

A web platform built to connect people who want to hike in Costa Rica with those who organize the walks.

It centralizes discovery, registration, and logistics for hikes, replacing today’s scatter across informal WhatsApp and Facebook groups.

The name comes from Quechua: the chaskis were messengers of the Inca Empire who traveled mountain routes connecting communities. A metaphor for the role hikers play today—and for the network Chaski aims to weave among them.
02 — Problem

Hiking organized across informal channels

Finding and organizing hikes in Costa Rica is fragmented:

  • 1 Information is scattered across Facebook groups, WhatsApp chats, and personal contacts.
  • 2 It’s hard to find hikes by difficulty, date, or location.
  • 3 WhatsApp lacks tools for logistics: capacity, recommended gear, confirmations, and reminders.
  • 4 Organizers share gear lists as loose images; participants have no structured way to check them off.
  • 5 There is no visible history or verifiable reputation for organizers or hikers.
03 — Solution

One platform to organize and discover

A web platform where:

  • ✓ The organizer publishes hikes, sets capacity, difficulty, location, and a recommended gear checklist.
  • ✓ The hiker discovers events, registers, checks off a personal gear list, and builds a history with badges.
  • ✓ Organizations (higher plans) can list hiking-related products in a classifieds-style storefront; transactions happen outside the app.
  • ✓ In a later phase: a conversational AI assistant with personalized recommendations based on the hiker’s profile and event details.
04 — Value proposition

Value for both sides

For the organizer

  • Reach verified hikers with your events
  • Structured capacity and registration management
  • Customizable gear checklist per event
  • Reputation and visibility on the platform
  • Product storefront (Pro / Enterprise plans)

For the hiker

  • Centralized hike discovery
  • Clear gear checklist per event
  • Verifiable hiking history
  • Achievement badges (gamification)
  • Filters by level, date, and location
